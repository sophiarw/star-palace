01 · Diagnosis

Three things broke
between prototype and prod.

01 · DPR

Sprites bake at 1×, screen draws at 2-3×.

Each typed sprite is rasterised once at logical pixel size. Retina renders, high-zoom passes, and Hi-DPI laptops all upsample that bitmap. Result: visible blockiness on every star bigger than a pinpoint.

canvas.width = half * 2 // logical, not DPR-aware
ctx.drawImage(sprite, x, y, drawScale, drawScale)
02 · Halo grading

Mid-stops are too wide; the disc reads translucent.

F15 already pulled outer alphas down to expose procedural detail — but the inner-mid range (12–45%) still spreads brightness across too much area. The "sun" looks like a fog patch.

addColorStop(0.45, alpha=0.40) // → 0.20
addColorStop(0.18, alpha=0.85) // → 0.95, tighter
03 · Smoothing

Wrong smoothing mode for each theme.

JWST baked sprites use low smoothing on upscale → soft. Vapor wants nearest for its hard pixel edges but inherits the same path. One pipeline, two opposite needs.

JWST: imageSmoothingQuality = 'high'
Vapor: imageSmoothingEnabled = false

02 · JWST direction

What we're chasing.

Stylised JWST — capture the feeling of NIRCam without trying to fake it. Deep cool background, color-temperature variation, crisp cores with exponential halos, dusty cyan/pink nebulosity at the layer below stars.

Color temperature

Stars span cool blues through amber/red. Never all-white. Per-id temperature bucket already exists; just needs to read in the right places.

Crisp cores, exponential halo

Hard, bright nucleus → fast falloff → long thin glow tail. Not gaussian fog.

Dusty teal nebulosity

Layer sits behind stars at very low alpha. Currently absent in-app.

Pink/red filaments

Hα-emission accent. Adds depth without coloring the stars themselves.

Near-black, never pure black

Background carries cool purple/maroon undertones. #07080d, not #000.

02 · JWST direction

Five principles.

01
Render at devicePixelRatio

Bake sprites at full DPR. No more "crisp at 1× / mush at 2×."

02
Tight halos, generous tails

Bright energy in the inner 18%; long thin falloff. Not fog.

03
Color temperature reads

Cool / neutral / warm — the bucket already exists. Push it harder so the star field doesn't read monochrome.

04
Sub-pixel diffraction

Spikes are 0.9–1.1 px linewidth at full DPR. Softer than a pixel, sharper than a glow.

05
Deep-field nebula behind

A subtle teal+pink wash painted under stars. Sells the depth.

ShowcaseProposed · main-sequence
A normal Sun-like star at high zoom. Crisp warm core, six-arm spikes, gentle pink+teal nebulosity behind, color-temp-varied micro-stars in the field.

03 · Vapor direction

Lean into the arcade.

The current vapor has the right palette but the wrong rendering pipeline. Going harder on 80s-arcade — pixel-art crispness, scanlines as part of the image, chromatic aberration on bright cores — solves the blur problem and the identity problem at the same time.

01
Nearest-neighbor, not bilinear

Vapor wants hard pixel edges. imageSmoothingEnabled = false on the upscale path. Posterized bands stay posterized.

02
Scanlines belong on every star

Currently background-only. Move them onto the sprite and the whole field reads as a single CRT.

03
Chromatic aberration as signature

2px magenta/cyan offset on bright cores. Costs nothing, sells the entire aesthetic.

04
Tron-grid horizon, not flat

Background gets perspective grid lines vanishing toward center. Already specced; we just paint it harder.

ShowcaseProposed · blue-supergiant
Cyan core, magenta outline, hyper-yellow X-spikes. Scanlines + chromatic aberration applied as the final pass.

04 · New surface

A second layer
under the stars.

Both themes get a far-background nebula wash painted between the canvas clear and the sprite blits. Costs one cached gradient texture per resize. Sells depth; makes empty regions of the map feel intentional rather than empty.

JWST · with nebula layer
Teal + dusty pink at ~10% alpha, painted before stars. Pinpoint micro-stars sprinkled at varying color temperatures.
Vapor · with grid + scanlines
Synthwave sunset gradient, Tron-grid horizon, CRT scanlines on top. Per-frame; cheap.

11 / 12Roadmap
The Ask

Decide where we cut.

All four stages are net-positive. The decision isn't whether to do them — it's whether we land them sequentially over four PRs or batch DPR + halo as a single F-NEXT-AB before touching nebula and CRT.

Recommendation:
land A + B
this week.

Stages 1 and 2 fix the actual visual regression — pixelation and blur. They share a test surface (sprite cache) and ship a believable in-app difference on day one. Stages 3 and 4 are aesthetic upgrades that can wait one cycle.

  • Confirm DPR-aware caching is OK with the 96 MB byte ceiling and 4000-entry cap (math in roadmap notes).
  • Pick a JWST nebula intensity — current proposal is 10% alpha; can go to 6% if it competes with stars.
  • Vapor scanline density — every 3px (proposed) vs every 4px (less aggressive)?
  • Land order: A → B → C → D, or batch?
  • Decide for me · Default = A+B this week
